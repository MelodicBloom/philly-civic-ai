## Repository-specific records

### D32 — Philly Civic AI: situated assistance with real community authority

**Use when:** When recovering civic personas, neighborhood planning tools, community dashboards, grants, or public-facing regenerative work.

[Recovered intent] The civic initiative proposes geographically/domain-specific assistance for community planning, preservation, anti-displacement, and regenerative infrastructure. Related concept briefs connect dashboards, textile loops, workshops, and immersive storytelling.

[Design DNA — proposed] Make sources, geography, date, uncertainty, and responsible human contacts visible. Offer plain-language meeting/needs-statement workflows alongside maps or explanatory visuals. Community knowledge should have room to correct the system.

[Observed boundary] Persona descriptions do not establish expert competence, representative legitimacy, current grant rules, or real partner relationships. The May opportunity brief includes speculative schedules and loosely supported calendar extensions; preserve the ideas, re-research factual premises before use.

[Repair resolution — proposed] Replace simulated community “voice” with scoped source-backed assistance and genuine participation. Separate illustrative data from live civic data, draft proposals from filed applications, and potential funders from confirmed eligibility. Keep neighborhood/community identities from being inferred from appearance or generalization.

[Reimagined horizon] A community-governed planning/learning toolkit that makes options and evidence legible and restores local decision capacity.

[Carry-forward test] A named use case produces an understandable draft with traceable current sources, explicit unresolved premises, and review by an appropriate participant. No generated persona acts as community approval.

## Practical continuation contract

1. Re-read current repository instructions and source before implementation. The baseline above identifies this publication, not a permanent execution HEAD.
2. Retain the full design horizon separately from the current demonstrator. Name what the demonstrator preserves, approximates, defers, or cannot prove.
3. Map tokens and controls to actual consumers with explicit units, ownership, accessible alternatives, and observable effect.
4. Complete the relevant interaction/error/cancellation states before promoting a reusable component.
5. Pair source/runtime checks with human correspondence review against the actual visual inspiration.
6. Keep proposed repairs unexecuted until a later implementation records its own revision, checks, and evidence.
